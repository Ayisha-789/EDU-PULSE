# EDU-PULSE

## Agentic AI for Adaptive Learning & Student Accountability

EDU-PULSE is an agentic AI learning companion designed to help students
plan their learning, stay accountable, verify their understanding, identify
weak concepts, and adapt their learning based on their actual performance.

Unlike a conventional study planner that only creates a timetable, EDU-PULSE
aims to continuously understand the student's learning state and take the
next appropriate action based on their progress, performance, and behaviour.

## What EDU-PULSE Does

- Creates personalized learning plans based on the student's goals and needs.
- Helps students study and revise specific topics.
- Tests whether the student actually understands the material.
- Evaluates performance at the concept/subtopic level.
- Identifies concepts that the student has not mastered.
- Retrieves relevant study material for targeted revision.
- Re-tests weak concepts instead of making the student repeat the entire topic.
- Adapts the learning flow based on the student's actual performance.
- Maintains student progress across sessions.
- Provides accountability and recovery mechanisms for missed learning tasks.

## Core Learning Loop

Understand → Plan → Study → Verify → Test → Analyse → Account → Adapt → Repeat
